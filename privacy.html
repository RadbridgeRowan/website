Privacy

How Radbridge collects and uses information on this website. Effective 11 November 2026.

Who we are

This website is operated by Radbridge Incorporated. For privacy questions, email rowan@radbridge.com or use our contact form.

Contact form

If you send a message through the contact form, we collect the name you provide (optional), your email address, and your message. The form is processed by Formspree, a service operated by Formspree, Inc. in the United States. Formspree forwards your submission to our email inbox.

We use that information only to reply to your enquiry and to keep a record of the conversation. We do not sell it or use it for advertising. We keep submissions for as long as they are useful for that correspondence, then delete them.

Google Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how people use the site (for example, which pages are visited and from which general regions). Google Analytics sets cookies such as _ga and _ga_HLPW1XP7RK. Google receives usage data and processes it under its own terms. You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and you can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

What we do not collect

We do not create user accounts on this website. We do not sell personal information. We do not use advertising cookies or remarketing tags.

Do Not Track

Some browsers send a Do Not Track signal. This website does not respond to that signal. Analytics and form handling work the same whether or not the signal is present.

Your requests

You can ask what personal information we hold about you from this website, or ask us to delete it, by emailing rowan@radbridge.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes

If we update this notice, we will change the effective date above.
