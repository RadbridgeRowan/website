FutureWeather

Global

Architects and engineers usually model building energy use and size HVAC systems with weather files based on past conditions, even though buildings will operate for decades in a changing climate. Radbridge developed FutureWeather to make climate-adjusted weather files quick to produce. Users upload a baseline EPW file and choose a future period, emissions scenario and climate models. The tool applies CMIP6 projections through a morphing method based on published research by Dr. Eugénio Rodrigues at the University of Coimbra, and returns EPW, DDY and STAT files in under a minute.

FutureWeather is available as a web app, a REST API and a Python package, with plug-ins for Rhino Grasshopper and Autodesk Revit through Dynamo. Hundreds of users have generated thousands of files for locations around the world. We presented FutureWeather to ASHRAE Technical Committee 4.2, which covers climatic information, in 2026.
