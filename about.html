About Radbridge

Radbridge is an economics consulting firm serving public agencies and their partners in parks, recreation, open space, and community resilience. We provide economic evidence to help them manage existing assets and evaluate new investments.

Leadership

Radbridge was founded in 2022 by Johnny Mojica and Rowan Schmidt.

Rowan Schmidt

Chief Executive Officer

Rowan has worked with public agencies for more than a decade, with a focus on effective and equitable investments in open space and community resilience. Areas of experience include benefit-cost analysis, federal policy, tool development, and funding and finance mechanisms.

rowan@radbridge.com

Johnny Mojica

Chief Operating Officer

Johnny specializes in measuring and communicating the benefits that parks and public spaces bring to communities. His work includes visitor use studies and economic impact analyses for park systems, along with benefit-cost analyses for community resilience investments.

johnny@radbridge.com
