Yolo Bypass Recreation Analysis

Yolo Bypass, California

The Sacramento Area Flood Control Agency wanted to understand recreation’s benefits in the Yolo Bypass and opportunities to improve existing recreation sites. Radbridge studied wildlife watching, hiking, hunting, and other outdoor activities across the bypass. We estimated visitation and visitor origins for 2023, then assessed visitor spending and the value people receive from recreation beyond what they spend.

Visitor spending supported an estimated $3.62 million in business activity and 20 jobs across Yolo, Sacramento, and Solano Counties. The report identifies opportunities to improve visitor amenities and outreach, particularly within the Yolo Bypass Wildlife Area. It gives agencies and partners information to consider recreation investments alongside the bypass’s flood management, agriculture, and habitat functions.
