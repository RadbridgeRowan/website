Parks, recreation & open space

We study how people use parks, trails, and open spaces to help agencies improve services and communicate the benefits these places bring to communities.

Our services

We work with park agencies, state recreation offices, natural resource managers, nonprofits, land trusts, and planning teams. From individual parks to statewide studies, we combine data science and economics to support planning, management, and investment decisions.

We share findings through reports, maps, concise summaries, and web-based tools you can use in budget and funding requests and public discussions.

Visitor use

Understand when people visit, where they come from, and how use varies within and across parks. Use visitation patterns and recreation interests to inform staffing, programming, and services.

Economic impact and contributions

Estimate how visitor spending, park operations, and capital investment contribute to jobs, income, and tax revenue.

Recreation and public benefits

Assess the value people receive from recreation beyond what they spend, along with benefits to public health, property values, and the environment.

Learn about recreation value studies →

Parkbeat

Parkbeat is our platform for bringing visitor information, survey findings, and park benefits together. We offer one-time reports, surveys, and dashboard access with updates as new data become available.
