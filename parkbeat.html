Overview

You receive an 8–10 page PDF with key findings and a limited-time dashboard login for basic exploration.

Who this is for

Park & Open-Space Managers

Need credible numbers for briefings or quick decisions

City & County Agencies

Preparing budget or grant materials

Conservancies & Nonprofits

Making the case for maintenance, improvements, or programming

What you receive

8–10 page PDF report with:

  • Visitation patterns (hourly, daily, monthly)
  • Typical length of stay (dwell time)
  • Visitor origins and travel distance
  • Neighborhood-based demographics (aggregated)
  • Clear notes on what the data suggests for operations and outreach
Dashboard access (introductory) to view the core charts for your park
Briefing-ready graphics suitable for boards, councils, and grant attachments

How it works

1

Define the park

Provide a boundary file or let us confirm/draw one with you.

2

Process the data

We run your site through our established mobile-location ETL pipeline. We filter pass-through traffic, remove obvious non-visitor signals, and aggregate results to protect privacy.

3

Deliver the results

We prepare the PDF and enable dashboard access. Most projects are completed within three business days of scope confirmation.

What the report shows

  • When people visit

    Peaks by hour and day; simple seasonality

  • How long they stay

    Dwell-time ranges

  • Where visitors come from

    Drive distance and origin areas

  • Who you're reaching

    Neighborhood-level demographics, aggregated

  • Manager takeaways

    Succinct notes about staffing windows, facility pressure points, communications, and grant language
