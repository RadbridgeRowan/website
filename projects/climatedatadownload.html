Climate Data Download

Global

NASA’s NEX-GDDP-CMIP6 dataset provides daily, downscaled climate projections for the whole globe, but using it means working with a 38-terabyte collection of NetCDF files. Radbridge developed Climate Data Download so engineers, planners, researchers and students can get projections for a single location without writing code. Users pick a point on a map, choose variables, climate models and emissions scenarios, and receive ready-to-use CSV or Excel files by email.

The files include daily temperature, precipitation, humidity, wind and radiation at about 25-kilometer resolution, for historical and future periods. The site also offers plain-language guides on choosing scenarios and models and on working with the data in Excel. The tool is free while in beta.
